## News

### [Docker Hub fixes critical flaw that passed access tokens to new owners](https://trustlist.uk/news/docker-hub-cve-2026-105445-converted-accounts-passed-access-tokens-to-new-owner)

Account-to-organisation conversion kept the old token secrets alive. Docker closed the hole in under four hours after a report and found no abuse across about 304,000 pushes.

### [Bricksforge WordPress plugin flaw lets anyone upload files, now exploited](https://trustlist.uk/news/bricksforge-wordpress-plugin-cve-2026-85097-cvss-10-file-upload-exploited-update-3-1-8-10)

CVE-2026-85097 scores CVSS 10 in Patchstack's database and affects every release up to 3.1.8.9. It follows a password-reset flaw fixed in September and a privilege-escalation bug disclosed earlier.

### [AWS bills AgentCore short-term memory per gigabyte instead of per event](https://trustlist.uk/news/aws-bedrock-agentcore-memory-short-term-pricing-moves-to-per-gb-billing)

Ingestion costs $1.00 per GB, retrieval $0.20 per GB and storage $0.10 per GB-month. AWS says most workloads will pay less, with small frequent events gaining most.

### [Google Ads API moves to two releases a month starting with v26](https://trustlist.uk/news/google-ads-api-two-releases-a-month-from-v26-v23-sunsets-february-2027)

Four major versions a year continue and each lasts twelve months, so v23 stops working in February 2027 and v26 in November 2027, with minors retired alongside their parent.

### [Upwind buys Israeli AI security start-up Aegis and opens an AI lab](https://trustlist.uk/news/upwind-acquires-aegis-israeli-ai-security-startup-launches-ai-lab)

No price, closing date or headcount is given. The new Upwind AI Lab will work on agent security, security benchmarks for frontier models and GPU protection.
