14 stories published on TrustList on Monday 12 October 2026: News, Funding news. Each item links to the full article on TrustList, with related rankings where we have them.

## News

### [Tencent closes its Yuanqi AI agent platform on 9 November](https://trustlist.uk/news/tencent-shuts-yuanqi-agent-platform-9-november-api-keys-stop-29-october)

New agents and API keys stop on 29 October; WeChat chatbots built on Yuanqi stop replying at the cutoff, and user data is deleted from 9 January 2027.

### [HashiCorp archives Boundary Community Edition, no patches planned](https://trustlist.uk/news/hashicorp-archives-boundary-community-edition-no-more-security-patches)

The open source access broker repository is read-only; development moves to HCP Boundary and Boundary Enterprise, and existing binaries stay downloadable but unmaintained.

### [DNS root zone now signed by new key KSK-2024, resolvers must trust it](https://trustlist.uk/news/dns-root-zone-switches-to-ksk-2024-resolvers-without-new-trust-anchor-may-return-servfail)

The previous root key, KSK-2017, signed from 2018 and has stopped; DNSSEC resolvers lacking key tag 38696 may fail every lookup, and the old key is due to be revoked in January 2027.

### [Europe: funding calls and deadlines closing soon, week of 12 October](https://trustlist.uk/news/europe-funding-calls-closing-week-of-2026-10-12)

Software, AI and cybersecurity calls closing from 19 October to 18 November across France, Germany, Ireland, Finland, Slovakia and EU programmes, with deadlines, eligibility and amounts as each funder states them.

### [GitLab patches CVSS 10 remote code flaw in its CI/CD regex compiler](https://trustlist.uk/news/gitlab-cve-2026-97229-cvss-10-regex-compiler-rce-upgrade-to-19-4-2-19-3-4-19-2-8)

The fix ships in patch releases 19.4.2, 19.3.4 and 19.2.8 alongside 23 other fixes, and single-node self-managed instances will see downtime during the upgrade.

### [Let's Encrypt moves its default certificate lifetime to 64 days](https://trustlist.uk/news/lets-encrypt-64-day-certificates-default-from-10-february-2027-update-renewal-timers)

Staging issues 64-day certificates from 14 October, production follows on 10 February 2027, and fixed-day renewal scripts will break; the 45-day default is expected in 2028.

### [PTA drafts passport checks for foreign SIMs and travel eSIMs](https://trustlist.uk/news/pta-draft-inbound-roaming-regulations-2026-passport-kyc-travel-esim-registration-comments-10-november)

The draft would also force travel eSIM sellers such as Airalo and Holafly to register with the regulator, cap permanent roaming at 90 days in 180 and ban M2M SIMs from consumer roaming.
