## News

### [Mistral Large 4 weights arrive with a paid licence for model hosts](https://trustlist.uk/news/mistral-large-4-weights-released-under-licence-that-requires-model-as-a-service-hosts-to-buy-commercial-terms)

Mistral kept its end-of-October promise, but the Ecosystem Open-Weight licence makes any business offering the model as a service sign a commercial deal first. Internal use stays free; API prices are $0.70 and $2.10 per million tokens.

<p class="related"><span class="label">Related on TrustList</span>: <a href="https://trustlist.uk/best/countries/france">Top B2B companies in France</a></p>

### [ICASA drafts biometric checks for every SIM activation and swap](https://trustlist.uk/news/icasa-draft-rules-biometric-check-for-every-sim-activation-and-swap-five-number-cap-per-operator)

The Numbering Plan Fifth Amendment would make South African operators verify new and existing subscribers biometrically and cap the numbers each may hold. Companies escape the fingerprint check but, by TechCentral's reading, not the cap.

<p class="related"><span class="label">Related on TrustList</span>: <a href="https://trustlist.uk/best/countries/south-africa">Top B2B companies in South Africa</a></p>

### [SBP makes SMEConnect the only route for SME loan applications](https://trustlist.uk/news/state-bank-of-pakistan-smeconnect-sole-channel-for-sme-financing-applications-from-1-november)

From 1 November 2026 Pakistani banks and DFIs take SME financing applications only through the State Bank's new portal, which shows applicants their status and rejection reasons and gives the regulator a live view of every lender.

<p class="related"><span class="label">Related on TrustList</span>: <a href="https://trustlist.uk/best/countries/pakistan">Top B2B companies in Pakistan</a></p>

### [Chile puts driver, plate and route data on every dispatch guide](https://trustlist.uk/news/chile-sii-resolution-144-transport-data-on-invoices-and-dispatch-guides-1-november-full-deadline-1-may-2027)

SII Resolution 144 replaces last year's Resolution 154. Issuers with the data comply from 1 November; outsourced transport gets until 1 May 2027, and every company must declare its transport model by 31 January.

### [Google and OpenAI both retire their custom assistant builders](https://trustlist.uk/news/google-gemini-gems-become-skills-17-november-openai-custom-gpts-retire-11-december-workspace-march-2027)

Gems go read-only on 17 November and vanish on 17 December for personal accounts, with Workspace following in March 2027. Custom GPTs retire on 11 December, and custom actions do not survive the move to plugins.

### [Brazil rewrites its SAC customer service rules and orders call signing](https://trustlist.uk/news/brazil-decree-13135-sac-rules-human-agent-20-second-wait-and-mp-1396-call-signing-for-operators)

Decree 13,135 replaces the 2022 SAC rules with a mandatory human option, a 20-second wait target and 48-hour fixes for undue charges. A provisional measure makes every operator sign calls and block unsigned ones.

## Security advisories

### [Chrome 156 update fixes critical MediaStream flaw and 19 high bugs](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/chrome-156-0-8078-79-fixes-21-flaws-critical-mediastream-memory-corruption-cve-2026-76024)

Google moved Stable and Extended Stable to 156.0.8078.79/.80 with 21 security fixes. One external researcher reported four of them, including the critical one; Google states no exploitation.

### [Linux TCP flaw CVE-2026-98432: Debian fixed, Ubuntu and RHEL waiting](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/linux-kernel-tcp-cve-2026-98432-use-after-free-debian-fixed-ubuntu-and-rhel-kernels-still-pending)

A race in IPv4 TCP connection setup frees socket options too early. The kernel CVE team scores it 9.8, distributions 5.9 to 8.1; Debian has fixed kernels, Ubuntu lists fixes in progress.

### [Mitel fixes OpenScape Endpoint Management bypass; Brotli fix planned](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/mitel-openscape-endpoint-management-node-red-auth-bypass-fixed-v1r2-5-1-brotli-cve-2026-111497-fix-planned)

MISA-2026-0009 closes a Node-RED admin bypass rated 8.8 in V1R2.5.1. MISA-2026-0010 says that release is still exposed to the Brotli decoder flaw, with dozens of Mitel products under investigation.

<p class="related"><span class="label">Related on TrustList</span>: <a href="https://trustlist.uk/best/countries/canada">Top B2B companies in Canada</a></p>

### [Dell fixes four CVSS 9.8 flaws in PowerProtect Data Domain](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/dell-powerprotect-data-domain-dsa-2026-416-four-cvss-9-8-flaws-fixed-dd-os-8-9-0-0-and-lts-releases)

DSA-2026-416 now covers unauthenticated root command execution, a hard-coded key and rogue admin creation in DD OS, plus three Management Center flaws that reach other managed systems. Fixes ship in 8.9.0.0 and three LTS lines.

### [CISA gives agencies until 1 November for GitLab and Cisco ASA flaws](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/cisa-adds-gitlab-cve-2026-97229-and-cisco-asa-ftd-cve-2026-76418-to-kev-due-1-november)

The GitLab regex flaw lets an unauthenticated user run code through a CI/CD configuration; the Cisco flaw reloads ASA and FTD firewalls. Both entered the exploited list on 29 October with a three-day deadline.

### [Drupal Commerce access bypass is exploited; update to 3.3.13](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/drupal-commerce-core-cve-2026-118539-exploited-access-bypass-fixed-3-3-13-and-8x-2-41)

SA-CONTRIB-2026-254 rates CVE-2026-118539 highly critical (21 of 25): no login needed, every configuration exposed and attacks already under way. The security team tells shops to audit their orders.

### [Fake coding extension in Cursor's marketplace planted an SSH key](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/malicious-claude-code-2-1-313-extension-in-cursor-marketplace-adds-ssh-backdoor-key-svc-telemetry)

A tampered Anthropic.claude-code 2.1.313 sat in the Cursor extension marketplace for about 11 hours on 29 October. Cursor says at most 5,000 users installed it and that uninstalling alone may not remove the backdoor.
