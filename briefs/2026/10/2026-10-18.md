## Security advisories

### [PgBouncer 1.26.1 fixes a SCRAM login bypass and two hang bugs](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/pgbouncer-1-26-1-fixes-scram-authentication-bypass-cve-2026-107350-and-two-dos-flaws)

A user who knows one SCRAM password can log in as any other SCRAM user, superusers included, on PgBouncer 1.17.0 to 1.26.0. Two further flaws let unauthenticated clients hang the pooler under specific settings.

### [psqlODBC 18.00.0005 fixes heap corruption from oversized parameter lists](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/psqlodbc-18-00-0005-fixes-heap-corruption-cve-2026-109448-more-than-32767-parameters)

Every release from 08.01.0102 to 18.00.0004 is affected. An application that builds IN lists from user input can be crashed, or left running on a corrupted heap.
