### [Microsoft Graph file APIs drop pre-authenticated URLs from April 2027](https://trustlist.uk/news/microsoft-graph-file-apis-stop-pre-authenticated-urls-from-1-april-2027)

SharePoint and OneDrive downloads, uploads, previews and thumbnails stop returning URLs with embedded auth; opt-in tenant controls for named app IDs come first.

### [HPE iMC authentication bypass (CVSS 9.1) is fixed in 7.3 E0713](https://trustlist.uk/news/hpe-imc-cve-2026-79842-authentication-bypass-cvss-9-1-fixed-in-7-3-e0713)
