## News

### [Bitwarden moves its app store builds to a commercial licence](https://trustlist.uk/news/bitwarden-app-store-and-download-builds-move-to-commercial-licence)

The GPLv3 clients stay on GitHub with today's features, but some future components will ship only in the commercial build. Self-hosting is unchanged; resellers are the target.

### [Fake "Security Audit" GitHub workflows now sweep git histories for keys](https://trustlist.uk/news/ghostaction-github-actions-security-audit-workflow-steals-secrets-from-git-history)

A new GhostAction wave used hijacked maintainer accounts, including the author of an Uber repository, to plant a credential-stealing workflow. Socket counts more than 500 accounts since 7 October.

### [Collibra buys Munich AI governance startup trail ML](https://trustlist.uk/news/collibra-acquires-trail-ml-munich-ai-governance-automation-eu-ai-act)

trail ML, founded in 2023, automates checks against the EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and the NIST AI framework. Collibra says it will re-run assessments as evidence changes and block agent actions that break policy.
