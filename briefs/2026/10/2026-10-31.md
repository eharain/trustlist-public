### [Dutch data regulator offers up to 35% off fines for admitted breaches](https://trustlist.uk/news/dutch-ap-enforcement-policy-up-to-35-percent-fine-reduction-for-admission-nine-to-eighteen-month-procedure)

The Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens has published its final enforcement policy after a consultation that drew 12 responses. Formal cases take nine to eighteen months on average, and the fine power lapses five years after a breach ends.

A server provider hosting its Armenia CDN node reported unauthorised access. Managed certificates are being replaced by 31 October; customers who upload their own certificates are told to revoke and reissue them.
