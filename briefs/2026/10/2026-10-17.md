### [Firefox 158 and ESR 153.5 fix sandbox escapes and dozens of memory bugs](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/firefox-158-esr-153-5-thunderbird-fix-sandbox-escapes-mfsa-2026-106-110)

CVE-2025-61624 lets an admin with write rights create or delete files through crafted CLI arguments on FortiOS, FortiPAM, FortiProxy and FortiSwitchManager. Older branches have no fix at all.
