## Security advisories

### [PostgreSQL fixes 52 flaws and ends support for version 14](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/postgresql-18-7-17-12-16-16-15-20-14-25-fix-52-security-flaws-postgresql-14-end-of-life)

The 12 November releases 18.7, 17.12, 16.16, 15.20 and 14.25 carry fixes for 52 vulnerabilities, most scored 8.8, and 14.25 is the last update version 14 will ever get. PostgreSQL 19 is due on 19 November.

### [Grafana fixes 11 flaws, three High, in folders and Git Sync](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/grafana-fixes-eleven-flaws-folder-move-privilege-escalation-and-git-sync-bypasses)

Grafana Labs published 11 advisories on 10 November; the three rated High at 7.1 let an Editor become Admin through a folder move, impersonate users across instances sharing a renderer token, or bypass folder rights in Git Sync.

### [Checkmk fixes three critical code-execution flaws; 2.3 gets none](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/checkmk-2-5-0p19-and-2-4-0p40-fix-three-critical-code-execution-flaws-2-3-gets-none)

Checkmk 2.5.0p19 and 2.4.0p40 fix three flaws scored 9.4 under CVSS 4.0, one reachable by any user allowed to edit rules in a folder; version 2.3.0 is affected but has reached end of life.

### [Mitel patches MX-ONE flaw giving LAN attackers PBX admin rights](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/mitel-mivoice-mx-one-misa-2026-0011-unauthenticated-admin-bypass-cvss-9-4-hotfixes-8-2)

### [Edito CMS fixes eight flaws, including JWT forgery to admin access](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/edito-cms-5-10-2607-fixes-eight-flaws-jwt-forgery-admin-takeover-cert-polska)
