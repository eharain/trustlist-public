## Security advisories

### [nginx 1.31.9 and 1.30.7 fix ten flaws, including HTTP/2 memory corruption](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/nginx-1-31-9-and-1-30-7-fix-ten-flaws-http-2-flow-control-memory-corruption-cve-2026-123007)

nginx released mainline 1.31.9 and stable 1.30.7 on 18 November 2026 with fixes for ten vulnerabilities, three rated major: HTTP/2 flow-control memory corruption, a TLS 1.3 request-body loop and a mail proxy use-after-free.

### [WordPress 7.1.5 security release fixes privilege escalation and SQL injection flaws](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/wordpress-7-1-5-security-release-template-part-block-privilege-escalation-sql-injection-fixes)

WordPress 7.1.5, released on 18 November 2026, fixes about 20 security issues, among them an unauthenticated privilege escalation through Template Part block attributes that could lead to remote code execution, and two SQL injection flaws.

### [Microsoft adds two SharePoint Server flaws left out of its November notes](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/sharepoint-server-subscription-edition-cve-2026-124223-rce-and-cve-2026-124222-added-after-november-updates)

Microsoft published CVE-2026-124223, a CVSS 8.8 remote code execution flaw in SharePoint Server Subscription Edition, and CVE-2026-124222 on 16 November 2026, saying both were fixed in the November updates but omitted from the notes.

### [Drupal core fixes critical installer takeover flaw in SA-CORE-2026-017](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/drupal-core-sa-core-2026-017-critical-installer-takeover-and-sa-core-2026-018-gadget-chain)

Drupal published SA-CORE-2026-017, a critical flaw that can let an attacker use install.php to reinstall a site and take it over, and SA-CORE-2026-018, a gadget chain, on 18 November 2026; releases 11.4.13, 11.3.22 and 10.6.22 came out that day.

### [Apache Roller XML-RPC flaw is being exploited and reaches back to version 4.0.0](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/apache-roller-xml-rpc-deserialization-cve-2026-129448-exploited-4-0-0-to-6-1-4-upgrade-to-6-1-7)

Apache says CVE-2026-129448, an unauthenticated deserialization flaw in the Roller blog server XML-RPC endpoint, is being actively exploited and affects 4.0.0 to 6.1.4, not only 6.1.5; the current fixed release is 6.1.7.

### [CISA adds Synology DSM, LiteLLM, REDCap and Apache Axis flaws to its exploited list](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/cisa-kev-18-november-synology-dsm-cve-2024-10441-litellm-cve-2026-40217-redcap-cve-2026-90817-apache-axis)

CISA added four exploited flaws to its KEV catalogue on 18 November 2026: Synology DSM CVE-2024-10441, LiteLLM CVE-2026-40217, REDCap CVE-2026-90817 and Apache Axis CVE-2026-90436, with federal deadlines of 21 November and 2 December.

### [FBI warns Gootloader used over 1,000 hacked WordPress sites to target legal professionals](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/fbi-flash-gootloader-over-1000-infected-wordpress-sites-216-domains-28-million-ransom-legal-searches)

An FBI FLASH of 16 November 2026 says it had identified more than 1,000 infected WordPress sites and 216 domains controlled by the Gootloader operator, linked to over US$28 million in ransomware payments, with lures aimed at US legal professionals.

### [Cisco fixes ten ClamAV flaws in Secure Endpoint connectors, rated High on Windows](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/cisco-secure-endpoint-connector-clamav-cve-2026-76411-and-nine-more-fixed-linux-1-29-3-mac-1-27-5-windows-8-6-4)

Cisco fixed ten ClamAV flaws, led by CVE-2026-76411, in its Secure Endpoint connectors: High on Windows (fixed in 8.6.4), Medium on Linux (1.29.3) and Mac (1.27.5). Attackers could stop scanning; no workarounds exist.

### [Splunk fixes critical SOAR code execution and Enterprise authentication bypass flaws](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/splunk-november-2026-advisories-soar-8-8-0-rce-cvss-9-9-enterprise-10-6-1-10-4-5-10-2-9-10-0-12-9-4-17-auth-bypass)

Splunk published its November 2026 advisories on 18 November: SOAR 8.8.0 fixes two remote code execution flaws (CVSS up to 9.9), and Enterprise 10.6.1, 10.4.5, 10.2.9, 10.0.12 and 9.4.17 fix two critical authentication bypasses.

### [Ruby 4.0.10, 3.4.13 and 3.3.14 fix seven regex and string flaws with RCE PoCs](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/ruby-4-0-10-3-4-13-3-3-14-fix-seven-regexp-and-string-case-conversion-flaws-rce-pocs-ruby-3-2-unfixed)

Ruby core released 4.0.10, 3.4.13 and 3.3.14 on 18 November to fix seven memory-safety flaws in the regular expression engine and string case conversion; it knows of PoCs that reach code execution. Ruby 3.2 and earlier get no fix.
