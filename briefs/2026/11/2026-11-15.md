## News

### [Italy fines Meta €805m over Facebook's Android gallery access](https://trustlist.uk/news/italy-garante-fines-meta-805-million-euros-facebook-android-gallery-suggestions-consent)

The Garante says the gallery suggestions feature, on by default since 2023, read photos and videos of about 20 million Italian Android users without specific consent. Meta has 60 days to change it.

<p class="related"><span class="label">Related on TrustList</span>: <a href="https://trustlist.uk/best/countries/italy">Top B2B companies in Italy</a></p>

### [Project Zero drops patch grace time and moves to 30-day disclosure](https://trustlist.uk/news/google-project-zero-full-90-disclosure-policy-60-days-february-2027-30-days-january-2028)

Since 2021 vendors had 90 days to fix plus 30 days for users to patch. Now every bug goes public 90 days after the report, falling to 60 days on 2 February 2027 and 30 days on 3 January 2028.

### [Study finds 24,664 Lovable-built sites hand out database rows](https://trustlist.uk/news/lovable-apps-expose-supabase-records-without-login-crissy-field-research)

A Hamburg security firm found records with email addresses in 7,403 of them, from passport numbers to chat logs, and says the 30% share is not a confirmed leak rate.

### [Chile charges WOM $2.6m for missing its 5G build milestone](https://trustlist.uk/news/chile-subtel-charges-wom-us-2-6-million-missed-5g-milestone-100-percent-due-31-december)

Subtel drew 62,749 UF from guarantees after WOM reached 53% to 68% of its committed 5G works by 30 September; the 100% deadline stays 31 December and charges total almost $70 million.
