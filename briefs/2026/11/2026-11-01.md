### [LXD and MicroCloud fix a flaw that lets a public certificate log in](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/lxd-microcloud-cve-2026-112816-client-certificate-chain-auth-bypass-fixed-6-10-1-5-21-9)

### [DebugBear tells RUM customers to remove its script after a DNS hijack](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/debugbear-rum-script-hijacked-via-registrar-dns-customers-told-to-remove-embed-code)

### [AWS ships kernel fixes for the TCP flaw and dates RDS and Redshift patches](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/aws-bulletin-2026-160-cve-2026-98432-amazon-linux-kernels-out-rds-aurora-patch-3-november)
