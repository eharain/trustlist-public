## Security advisories

### [Apache Tomcat clusters on TLS-PSK and TLS 1.3 let unauthenticated nodes join](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/apache-tomcat-cve-2026-133325-cluster-tls-psk-tls-1-3-does-not-validate-peer-critical-fixes-not-yet-released)

A critical Tomcat flaw lets an unauthenticated node join a cluster secured with TLS 1.3 and TLS-PSK; the fixed 9.0.125, 10.1.63 and 11.0.30 are not yet released, so the project advises TLS 1.2 for now.

### [FreeBSD publishes 21 security advisories in one day, including NFSv4 and SNMP remote flaws](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/freebsd-21-security-advisories-17-november-2026-nfsv4-remote-code-execution-bsnmpd-local-root-fixed-15-1-p7-14-5-p4)

FreeBSD released 21 advisories on 17 November 2026, from an NFSv4 server use-after-free and an unauthenticated bsnmpd hole to local root bugs; fixes are in 15.1-RELEASE-p7, 14.5-RELEASE-p4 and 14.4-RELEASE-p13.

### [GPGME 2.3.1 fixes a critical double free reachable through a crafted OpenPGP key](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/gpgme-2-3-1-fixes-critical-double-free-cve-2026-129975-crafted-openpgp-user-id-gpg4win-mail-clients)

GnuPG released GPGME 2.3.1 on 20 November 2026 to fix CVE-2026-129975, a double free triggered by a key with a very long user ID; Gpg4win, GnuPG Desktop, most mail clients and Samba use the library.

### [GL.iNet says a cloud management flaw was used to plant malware on some routers and KVMs](https://trustlist.uk/security-advisories/glinet-cloud-remote-management-flaw-used-to-install-malware-on-routers-and-kvms-security-firmware-22-november)
