# How TrustList ranks

A short summary of the method. The full version, with every criterion, weight and scale, is at
[trustlist.uk/best/methodology](https://trustlist.uk/best/methodology).

## One score, from evidence

Every ranking orders listings by the TrustList Score: one number out of 100, built from evidence about
what clients say, what has been checked, and what the business commits to.

- A listing gets a rank number only when something about it has been checked. Until then it is listed,
  not ranked.
- Facts a business states on its own website count as evidence. TrustList reads the site, records the
  fact with the date it was read, and the business can confirm or correct it from its profile.
- How much an answer counts depends on how it is known: stated by the business, checked by TrustList
  or confirmed through vetting, or measured directly (uptime, releases, response to enquiries).
- Each entry says why it is where it is, and what is not yet on record.

## Quarterly editions

Rankings are published four times a year as dated editions, ordered on the evidence held on a stated
cut-off date, and then fixed until the next edition. That is what makes a place citable: a rank badge
names the edition it comes from. The first edition is
[Q4 2026](https://trustlist.uk/best/editions/2026-Q4); all editions are at
[trustlist.uk/best/editions](https://trustlist.uk/best/editions).

When scores are equal, the listing with more checkable facts comes first, then the one with the longer
track record. Listings equal on all three share a place.

## What money does not buy

Being listed, ranked, vetted or entered for an award never costs anything. Sponsored placements are
labelled and shown apart from the ranking, and paid services never add a point.

## Checking a badge

Every rank badge links to a verify page that shows the listing, the ranking and the edition it cites.
The [`@trustlist/badge`](../packages/badge) component in this repository shows a badge on a business's
own site and links to the ranking it comes from.
